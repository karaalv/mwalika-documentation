# Mwalika Road Map

## Overview

This document outlines the strategic road map for the Mwalika project, detailing the key phases, timelines, and milestones required to deliver a credible MVP and a compelling pitch.

The road map is structured into four key phases:

1. **Phase 1: Requirements and Planning**
2. **Phase 2: Development and Testing**
3. **Phase 3: Pitch Completion**
4. **Phase 4: Refinement and Rehearsal**

The project is developed explicitly from a pitch-driven perspective. As such, each phase is aligned not only with technical delivery, but also with the progressive construction of a clear, defensible, and persuasive pitch narrative. This ensures that feasibility, impact, and execution credibility are demonstrated alongside functional implementation.

The project is led and executed by a single individual, who is solely responsible for the design, development, documentation, and delivery of the final pitch. Engagement with relevant stakeholders, domain experts, and advisors will be undertaken as needed to validate assumptions and strengthen content quality.

## Phase 1: Requirements and Planning

**Dates**: 26 January – 30 January

The objective of this phase is to establish a clear, well-justified foundation for the Mwalika project. This includes defining the problem space, articulating the proposed solution, and outlining a feasible technical architecture.

During this phase, the first three sections of the pitch-writing document will be drafted:

1. **Background Research and Justification**  
   A comprehensive overview of the need for Mwalika in the Kenyan context, supported by global precedents and local challenges.

2. **Proposed Solution and MVP Scope**  
   A clear articulation of the Mwalika system, including core capabilities, MVP boundaries, and feature prioritisation rationale.

3. **Technical Architecture and Feasibility**  
   A high-level technical design covering system components, technology stack, data governance, and implementation approach, with particular emphasis on privacy, security, scalability, and digital sovereignty.

This phase ensures that scope, assumptions, and constraints are explicitly defined before development begins.

### Milestones

1. Completion of pitch-writing document sections 1–3  
2. Creation of a detailed project plan outlining tasks, timelines, and more detailed milestones  
3. Identification of key stakeholders and initial engagement strategies related to eCitizen and government services  
4. Finalisation of the MVP scope and proposed system architecture

Stakeholder engagement is expected to continue throughout the project lifecycle and is not limited to this phase.

### Deliverables

This phase is considered complete once the following deliverables are finalised. The key below indicates priority:

- **Completed pitch-writing document sections 1–3** (MUST)  
- **Detailed project plan** (MUST)  
- Defined MVP scope and technical architecture document (MUST)  
- Initial stakeholder engagement summary (SHOULD)

Progression to Phase 2 is not permitted until all MUST deliverables are complete.

## Phase 2: Development and Testing

**Dates**: 31 January – 27 February

The objective of this phase is to build and validate a functional MVP of the Mwalika system in line with the defined scope and architecture.

This includes implementation of core agent functionality, development of mock or simulated integrations with existing government systems where required, and structured testing to validate usability and reliability.

While development is the primary focus, the pitch-writing document will be iteratively updated to reflect technical progress, refined assumptions, and any justified scope changes.

### Milestones

1. Completion of a functional Mwalika alpha and MVP aligned with the Phase 1 scope  
2. Testing and validation of key user flows, including limited user feedback where feasible  
3. Iterative updates to pitch-writing document sections 1–3  
4. Setup of hosting and deployment infrastructure consistent with the proposed architecture, with an emphasis on future scalability and security

Development will follow an iterative approach, prioritising end-to-end reliability over feature breadth.

### Deliverables

This phase is considered complete once the following deliverables are finalised:

- **Completed Mwalika MVP** (MUST)  
  - The MVP must satisfy the agreed scope from Phase 1. If constraints arise, scope adjustments must be explicitly justified and documented.
- **Testing and validation report** (MUST)  
- Updated pitch-writing document sections 1–3 reflecting implementation reality (SHOULD)

## Phase 3: Pitch Completion

**Dates**: 28 February – 13 March

The focus of this phase is to finalise the pitch-writing document and translate it into a clear, coherent, and persuasive pitch presentation.

The completed pitch document will serve as the authoritative reference for the project, covering strategic intent, technical feasibility, societal impact, and economic viability. The pitch presentation will be developed in parallel, ensuring alignment between written content, visuals, and live delivery.

During this phase, the remaining sections of the pitch-writing document will be completed:

1. **User Adoption and Behavioural Change Strategy**  
2. **Stakeholder Engagement and Governance**  
3. **Risk, Legal and Ethical Considerations**  
4. **Monitoring, Impact Measurement and KPIs**  
5. **Cost Benefit Analysis**

### Milestones

1. Completion of pitch-writing document sections 4–8  
2. Review and refinement of the full pitch document for clarity, coherence, and impact  
3. Finalisation of the pitch script, demo narrative, and supporting materials  
4. Development of the pitch presentation, including visual structure, a pre-recorded system demo, and any supporting multimedia assets

### Deliverables

This phase is considered complete once the following deliverables are finalised:

- **Completed pitch-writing document sections 4–8** (MUST)  
- **Completed pitch presentation** (MUST)  
- **Finalised pitch script and supporting materials** (MUST)

## Phase 4: Refinement and Rehearsal

**Dates**: 14 March – 20 March

The objective of this phase is to ensure confident, controlled, and professional delivery of the pitch.

No new features or content will be introduced during this phase. All effort is directed towards rehearsal, timing, clarity, and risk mitigation during live delivery.

### Milestones

1. Completion of multiple pitch rehearsals, including timing and delivery refinement  
2. Final adjustments to presentation materials based on rehearsal feedback  
3. Preparation of contingency responses for likely questions, technical failures, or challenges

### Deliverables

There are no new artefacts produced in this phase. Readiness is defined by completion of the following activities:

- **Completed pitch rehearsals** (MUST)  
- **Finalised pitch presentation and supporting materials** (MUST)  
- **Prepared contingency plans for pitch delivery** (MUST)

## Note on Execution Risk

This project carries inherent execution risk due to its delivery by a single individual. To mitigate this risk:

1. The MVP scope will be deliberately constrained to ensure feasibility within time and resource limits. Scope reductions will be prioritised over schedule slippage if necessary.
2. Regular internal checkpoints will be used to assess progress, identify blockers, and course-correct early.
3. Ongoing engagement with relevant stakeholders and domain experts will be used to validate assumptions and improve content quality.
4. Contingency plans will be maintained for critical phases, particularly development, demo stability, and pitch delivery.

This approach ensures that the project remains realistic, defensible, and resilient under time pressure.
