# Pitch Stage 1 Plan

**Focus**: Strategic alignment to national security/resilience and initial execution quality.

## Pitch Narrative

- The goal is to convice the judges:
  
1. The problem of digital government services in Kenya is significant and has a real impact on citizens
2. Mwalika is a well-designed solution that addresses the specific challenges faced by Kenyan citizens in accessing digital government services
3. The vision for Mwalika is much bigger and can scale well beyond the current version to become a critical part of Kenya's digital government ecosystem.

**Problem**: Kenya is working toward a digital government, but many citizens struggle to access and navigate the available online services, leading to inefficiencies and inequities in service delivery.

**Impact**: People rely on intermediaries, which can be costly and lead to exploitation. This limits access to essential services for those who are not digitally literate, and hinders efficiency for those who are.

**Solution**: Mwalika is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed to help Kenyan citizens navigate digital government services, providing personalized guidance and support to ensure equitable access for all.

**Vision**: In the future, Mwalika will be an integral part of Kenya's digital government ecosystem, providing a fully agentic system that can proactively assist citizens with a wide range of government services, improving efficiency and accessibility for all.
