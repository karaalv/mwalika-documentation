# Mwalika Pitch - Background Research and Justification

## Similar Projects Globally

### Estonia - Burokratt

- Estonia is a global leader in digital government services, with a highly advanced e-governance system. The e-government (e-Estonia) platform provides a wide range of services, from voting to tax filing, all accessible online.
  - This system is highly efficient, user-friendly, and secure, making it easy for citizens to access government services digitally.
  - In general, Estonia's digital government services have been praised for their accessibility and efficiency.
- Burokratt is an AI-powered virtual assistant that helps citizens navigate Estonia's digital government services.
- Pros:
  - Very effective in a highly digital-savvy population.
  - Strong government support and infrastructure.
- Cons:
  - May not be directly applicable to countries with lower digital literacy or infrastructure.
  - The systems is not like contemporary large language models, so it may have limitations in understanding complex queries.
  - Heavily text based with limited voice interaction capabilities.

#### References

- e-Estonia: <https://e-estonia.com/>
- Burokratt: <https://www.ria.ee/en/state-information-system/personal-services/burokratt>

**Lessons from Estonia**:

- Strong digital infrastructure and government support are crucial for the success of digital government services.
- AI works best when built along side a well-established digital ecosystem, not as a standalone solution.

### UAE - TAMM

- The UAE has made significant strides in digital government services through its TAMM platform, which offers a wide range of services to citizens and residents, it is considered the 'one stop shop' for government services in the UAE.
- TAMM integrates various government services into a single platform, making it easier for users to access, this has millions of users and has crossed an overall satisfaction rate of 90%.
- TAMM features lots of personalisation options, allowing users to tailor their experience based on their needs, they follow a 'journey' based approach to service delivery, making sure users can easily find and complete tasks.
- Implements **proactive approach** with AI, giving users reminders and updates about important tasks and deadlines before they even ask (e.g., renewing licenses, paying bills).
- Abu Dhabi has spent over $3 billion on digital transformation initiatives, showcasing strong government commitment.
- Pros:
  - Comprehensive integration of services.
  - High user satisfaction.
- Cons:
  - The app is primarily designed for a tech-savvy population, which may not be the case in all regions.

## Why Mwalika is Needed in Kenya

- eCitizen is Kenya's primary digital platform for accessing government services, with over 22,000 services available online it is a significant step towards digital governance.
- However, challenges remain:
  - Digital literacy rates are still relatively low, especially in rural areas.
  - Many citizens face difficulties navigating the eCitizen platform due to its complexity.
- This results in:
  - High reliance on intermediaries like cybercafes, which can lead to increased costs and potential exploitation.
  - Limited access to government services for those who are not digitally literate.
  - Reduced trust in digital government services.
- This creates a structural barrier to national progress: the citizens who would
benefit most from efficient, low-cost digital services are often the least able to access
them.

- Other nations have already begun implementing AI-driven solutions to enhance digital government services, demonstrating the potential benefits of such systems.
- In order for Kenya to fully realize the benefits of digital governance, a system like Mwalika is essential to bridge the gap between citizens and digital services.
- This is not just a matter of convenience; it is a critical step towards ensuring equitable access to government services for all citizens, regardless of their digital literacy levels.
- Broader access to government services can lead to improved social outcomes, increased economic participation, and enhanced trust in public institutions, all of which are vital for national development.
  
## Unique Challenges and Opportunities in the Kenyan Context

**Challenges**:

- Digital literacy remains a significant barrier, particularly in rural areas where access to technology and the internet may be limited, it is essential that Mwalika is designed with user-friendliness and accessibility in mind to ensure it can be effectively used by all citizens no matter their level of digital literacy.
- Access to the internet also remains a challenge in certain regions, which could limit the reach of Mwalika, strategies to improve internet access and affordability will be crucial to the success of the system.
- The current technical landscape for digital government services is spread across multiple platforms and systems, each managed by different government agencies, this fragmentation could pose challenges for integration and interoperability, requiring careful planning and coordination among various stakeholders.

**Opportunities**:

- Unlike many other nations, Kenya already has a unified digital platform (eCitizen) for government services, which provides a solid foundation for Mwalika to build upon.
- This allows Kenya to leapfrog some stages of digital infrastructure development that other countries had to go through, and emerge as a leader in AI-driven digital governance in Africa which could set a precedent for further technological advancements in the nation.
- Streamlined access to government services would save the government money in the long run by reducing the need for physical infrastructure and in-person support services, this allows these resources to go towards other critical areas of national development such as healthcare and education.

## Justification for Mwalika

- Mwalika addresses a critical need in Kenya by providing an AI-driven solution to enhance access to government services, particularly for those with low digital literacy.
- By simplifying the user experience and providing personalized assistance, Mwalika has the potential to significantly improve digital access for Kenyan citizens
- This aligns strongly with the theme of "AI for National Prosperity". By widening digital inclusion, Mwalika improves economic empowerment through easier business registration, tax filing, and access to social services.
- The result is a more resilient, inclusive, and efficient public service ecosystem, one that ensures all citizens can participate fully in Kenya’s digital future
